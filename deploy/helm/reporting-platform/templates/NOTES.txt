Check the init job (runs `airflow pools set lakehouse_write` and
`registry schema` -- docs/DECISIONS.md#airflow-init-load-bearing-steps):

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs job/{{ .Release.Name }}-platform-init

A SKIPPED or FAILED job here means the `lakehouse_write` pool never got
created, and every DAG task will sit `queued` forever with nothing in its own
log to say why (docs/DECISIONS.md#one-shared-write-pool).

Airflow webserver:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ .Release.Name }}-webserver 8080:8080

A new platform image needs the release upgraded (`helm upgrade` with the new
digest) -- and rolls the inbox pod along with everything else, which is
required, not incidental: see this chart's README, "A new platform image
rolls the inbox pod".
